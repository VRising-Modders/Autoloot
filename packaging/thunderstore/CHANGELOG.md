# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Drops within the pickup range go into your bags: loot from kills, resource nodes and anything else on the ground. Gear and jewels keep their stats.
- Pickup notices: what you picked up and how many of each you now carry, such as `Bone +20 (100)`.
- **Show my pickup range** draws the range as a ring of flames on the ground; it can have a key.
- Per player: autoloot on or off, a pickup filter (the most of each item to carry, 0 for never), pickup notices.
- Server settings in the game: range (1 to 30 m), what is picked up, how several players share, death bags, dropped inventory bags, and players without the client part.
