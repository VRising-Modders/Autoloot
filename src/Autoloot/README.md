# Autoloot

Drops near you go straight into your bags: loot from kills, resource nodes and anything else that lands on the ground. Needs Progenitor on the server; install it on clients too for the settings and the pickup filter.

**Admins** choose, on this page:

- whether players may use autoloot at all,
- the pickup range (1 to 30 m, default 10),
- what is picked up: any drop that comes in range, or only drops that land while the player is in range,
- who gets a drop when several players are in range: the closest player, or players take turns,
- death bags: never, only the player's own (default), or anyone the game lets open them,
- dropped inventory bags: never (default), or anyone except the player who dropped them.

**Players** turn autoloot on or off for themselves, and keep a pickup filter: items with the most of each they want in their bags (0 = never).

**With Satisvampory** on the same server, autoloot can stop at your island's caps, and your filter can hold Satisvampory categories (such as Flowers or Materials).

Everything else that drops is picked up, gear and jewels included with their stats. Never picked up: items a player dropped, and soul shards.

Turn on **Show my pickup range** to see the range drawn on the ground around you. **Help** opens a guide with the same range drawn to scale.
